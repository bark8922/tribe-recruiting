# New Role Briefing — the idea in one page

**What it is:** when a new role opens in Bubble, the TA and sourcer on it automatically get one Slack DM within a few hours. It pulls everything we already know from similar roles we've run before, so nobody starts a role cold.

**Where the numbers come from:** our own history. Time-to-hire from every comparable past role, the real reasons candidates dropped, whether hires came from sourcing or inbound applicants, who on the team has closed this kind of role before, and any intake call notes we have. Nothing is generated or guessed; it's all counted from our data.

**How "similar" works:** same role type, same market. A backend engineer in Berlin is compared against backend roles in Germany/DACH, not Tokyo. The message always says how many roles it's based on.

---

## What the DM looks like (real data, real test)

> 🆕 **New role: Marketing Manager, Germany**
> Based on **36 similar roles** (Marketing, Germany) since 2022, mostly Wolt (14), Taxfix (8), Eucalyptus (4).
>
> **⏱ Benchmarks**
> Median time-to-hire: **38 days**. Fast quartile ~23, slow 47+. First qualified candidate around **day 16**. Heads up: German marketing runs slower than our overall book.
>
> **🚫 What kills candidates here**
> **Skills (26%)**, Location (16%), **Salary (14%)**. Contract type shows up too (7%), so nail down perm-vs-contract and comp early.
>
> **📥 Where hires actually come from**
> Unlike tech roles, **applicants beat sourcing here: 9 of 16 hires were inbound**. Push the job ad hard; the pipeline fills from the posting.
>
> **👥 Who's done this before**
> **Marina Nikolic: 8 of 16 hires** (Taxfix, Berlin). Then Alisa Liddell (3), Rossella Petrillo (2).
> ⚠️ **2 possibly overlapping roles are open right now** (Wolt B2B marketing, Berlin, Elena).

The same test on "Backend Engineer, Berlin" told the opposite story: candidates die on location and remote-only, 57% of hires are sourced, and the go-to people are Simon and Vladimir. That's the point: the advice changes per role, and it's waiting in your DMs before you've made your first call.
