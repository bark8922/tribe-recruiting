# Weekly Roles Update — Sourcer Guide

_Rolling out Monday. This is the pack to review and send to the sourcing team._

---

## Part 1 — Announcement message (paste into the sourcer channel / group DM)

Hey team 👋

Starting Monday, you'll get a short weekly check-in from the **Roles Weekly Update** bot to make it easy to share where each of your roles stands. Here's the deal:

**What you'll get.** Every weekday at **16:30 CET**, the bot DMs you a roundup of your active roles for the week, each already showing your sourcing numbers (contacted, positive responses, response rate). It's the same DM each day, refreshed with the latest numbers and whatever you've already filled in.

**What to do.** For each role, tap the button and add a quick update: pick a status, tell us what you tried this week, and flag anything that's stuck. It takes a minute or two per role.

**Draft vs submit.**
- **Save as draft** = private, just for you, editable all week.
- **Submit** = locks it in for the week. For roles with a Tribe TA, submitting sends them your update. For roles where you work directly with the client (you're effectively the TA), submitting just records it for the weekly review — it doesn't ping anyone.

**Deadline & the Monday safety net.** Please aim to have everything submitted by **end of day Friday**, that's the ask. If you forget, you'll get a reminder on **Monday at 9:00 CET** listing whatever's still outstanding, so you get a last chance. Then at **12:00 CET Monday**, anything still not submitted is finalized for you automatically: if you saved a draft, it gets submitted as-is (no penalty, you did the work); if it's blank, the TA just gets your numbers with a "no written update" note. Bottom line: Friday is the goal, Monday noon is the hard cutoff, and your numbers go through either way, but it's much better to add the context yourself.

**Why we're doing it.** A few reasons. It keeps delivery aligned and surfaces blockers early, while they're still fixable. It cuts down the back-and-forth with your TA, instead of pinging them throughout the week, this becomes one clean weekly update. And the bigger picture: we're building a real database of sourcing strategy, blockers, and market insight for each role, so we can learn from what's working and actually act on it over time. The goal is to start putting that data to use as soon as it's flowing, and eventually surface it in the dashboard so anyone can look up the story behind a role. For now, the priority is simply capturing it.

Full field-by-field guide below. Shout if anything's unclear 🙏

## Part 2 — How to fill it out (field-by-field)

When you tap a role, a short form opens. Here's what each part means.

**Status this week** — pick one:
- 🟢 **On track** — no major issues. Keeps the blocker and risk fields optional.
- 🟡 **Some friction** — you want to flag something. Makes blocker + risk required.
- 🔴 **Blocked** — you need help. Makes blocker + risk required.

New roles open on 🟢 by default, so if it's on track you can just write your update and submit.

**🎯 Sourcing direction for this role** _(optional)_ — where you're focusing your search. Saved to the role, so you don't have to retype it each week.

**🏁 Target outcome** _(optional)_ — what success looks like for this role.

**Where are candidates dropping off / biggest blocker?** — required if status is 🟡 or 🔴. Where in the funnel you're losing people, or what's holding the role up.

**What's the risk if this continues, and what would you change?** — required if 🟡 or 🔴. Looking ahead: what could derail this, and your recommendation.

**What did you try this week?** — the main field, please always fill this in. Walk through the chain, e.g. _"This week I tried A because of B, which resulted in C, and next I'll try D."_

**What do you want to do?**
- 📤 **Submit** (default) — locks it in for the week; sends to the TA where there is one.
- 📝 **Save a private draft** — keep working on it; not counted until you submit.

## Part 3 — Quick FAQ

**Do I have to fill in every role every day?** No. The DM refreshes daily so you can add notes when a role is fresh in your mind, but you only need each role submitted once by Monday 9:00 CET.

**I work embedded with a client — does my TA see this?** No. For roles where you're the point of contact with the client, your submission is just recorded for the internal weekly review. Nothing goes to an external contact.

**Can I edit after submitting?** Yes, until Monday 12:00 CET when the week locks. Editing a submitted role re-notifies the TA if something changed.

**What if I saved a draft but forgot to submit?** No problem, on Monday at 12:00 we submit your saved draft for you automatically. Blanks (nothing written) are the only thing that goes to the TA as numbers-only.

**The form won't open / spins forever.** Fully quit and reopen Slack, then try again. If it still happens, message Blake.
