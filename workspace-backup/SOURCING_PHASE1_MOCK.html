METHODOLOGY

Numbers reflect internal sourcing work only. A sourcer's contact/screen/hire counts toward the team if either: (a) they were on Bench/Internal in BambooHR at the event date, OR (b) they were billed to a client but sourced for a different client (cross-client sourcing). client work (sourcer billed to client X and sourced for client X) is excluded.
